The Spectrum of Heat Illness

This section provides a high-level overview of heat-related illness, from its underlying pathophysiology to the clinical spectrum. Understanding this progression is key to recognising the severity of a patient's condition and the urgent need for intervention in cases of heat stroke. Heat illness is not a single diagnosis but a continuum of severity reflecting the body's escalating failure to control its core temperature.

Pathophysiology: Why Heat Stroke is a Systemic Disease

Heat stroke is not simply a state of being too hot; it is a profound systemic inflammatory disease akin to severe sepsis. Extreme heat triggers cellular damage and compromises the gut barrier, leading to endotoxin release. This unleashes a "cytokine storm" and a Systemic Inflammatory Response Syndrome (SIRS), which drives multi-organ failure. This is why antipyretics like paracetamol are ineffective and contraindicated—the issue isn't the brain's thermostat, but an out-of-control peripheral inflammatory cascade.

Triage & Primary Survey (ABCDE)

Rapid and accurate assessment is critical. During heatwaves, maintain a high index of suspicion. The primary survey must focus on identifying the life-threatening features of heat stroke, particularly CNS dysfunction. Remember to get a proper core temperature measurement early.
